ECET 231 Electrical Power & Machinery — Motor Intuition Manual

A reconstruction of the Purdue University North Central ECET 231 Electrical Power and Machinery Lab Manual (Summer 2012), rebuilt as an interactive engineering reference. The end goal is a complete motor datasheet produced from the manual’s tests — either to characterize a motor you have, or to specify one for an application.

New in v2.5 — Motor Models: brushed DC and BLDC as one model, built up as differential equations: the LR armature with back-EMF and mechanics first, then the LRC model (winding capacitance, self-resonance), then more parasitics (brush drop, cable, saturation, cogging); thermals as first-order and two-node ODEs (heating, cooling, duty cycle, electro-thermal runaway); BLDC six-step vs FOC; and new LCR-meter / VNA tests that feed the datasheet. In all three editions.
